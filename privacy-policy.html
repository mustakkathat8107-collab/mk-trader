MK Trader – Privacy Policy
Last update: 11 February, 2026

Your privacy is important to us.

1. Purpose of the App

MK Trader is an educational application only. It provides trading-related learning content for educational purposes. MK Trader does not provide financial, investment, or legal advice. No profit or income is guaranteed. Users are fully responsible for how they use the information provided in the app.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the user's mobile number for login and authentication purposes. We do not collect name, email address, contacts, SMS content, call logs, location data, device media, or any personal files stored on the user's device.

3. Use of Data

The collected mobile number is used strictly for identity verification, session management, security purposes, and access control for purchased content. We do not sell, rent, or share user data with unauthorized third parties.

4. Payments

All payments made within the MK Trader app are processed only through Google Play Billing. No external or third-party payment systems are used. MK Trader does not store any card details, UPI information, or bank-related data. Payment handling and security are fully managed by Google Play.

5. Refund Policy

All purchases made within the app are final. Once a payment is successfully completed, it is strictly non-refundable under any circumstances. Any refund-related policies are governed by Google Play’s refund policy.

6. Account Access & Deletion

MK Trader provides users with full control over their account. Users can delete their account directly from inside the app using the available account delete option. Once an account is deleted, all associated user data is permanently removed and access to purchased content is revoked. Account deletion is irreversible.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

User data is retained only while the account is active. When a user deletes their account, the associated data is deleted from our systems within a reasonable time. We do not retain unnecessary or inactive user data.

8. Data Security

User data is securely stored using Firebase and Google services. Industry-standard security practices are implemented to protect user information. However, no method of data transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. Third-Party Services

MK Trader may use trusted third-party services such as Google Firebase, Google Analytics, and Google Play Services. These services may collect limited information as per their respective privacy policies.

10. Notifications

We may send notifications related to app updates, important information, account activity, or purchase-related messages. Users can disable notifications at any time from their device or app settings.

11. Children's Privacy

MK Trader is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If such data is identified, it will be deleted immediately.

12. User Consent

By using the MK Trader application, users agree to this Privacy Policy and consent to the collection and use of information as described.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

MK Trader reserves the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be reflected on this page. Continued use of the app implies acceptance of the updated policy.

14. Legal Jurisdiction

This Privacy Policy shall be governed by and interpreted in accordance with the laws of India.

MK Trader delivers premium course content through a secure, proprietary in-app video streaming player. All course videos are hosted on encrypted, high-performance cloud infrastructure and are protected against unauthorized downloading, screen recording, or external sharing. These videos play exclusively inside the MK Trader application and are accessible only to authorized users who have successfully enrolled in or purchased the relevant course.

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising Identifier)

All data collection and ad personalization are governed by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

MK Trader does not guarantee uninterrupted availability of rewarded ads. Ad availability depends on Google AdMob inventory, region, and network conditions.
